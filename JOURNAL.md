## a very short Q&A
### what is mallow? 
it's gonna be a little handheld game console inspired by the gameboy that uses real cartridges since i love physical media
### what games are going to be made for it?
whatever games you want! i plan on making an SDK so anyone can program in Lua to make whatever game they so desire.
### what do i want to get out of this?
3 things: 
**knowledge**: i want to learn the basics of electrical engineering before i head off to college in a few years. 
**a community**: i always wanted to make something people were actually interested in! 
**cool games to play**: im tired of these boring big-name company games! give me something indie to play!
